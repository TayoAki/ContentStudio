# Microsoft Fabric: what's performing (Oct 21 2026)

**The finding:** Fabric content that wins on short-form is useful before it's clever:
- **Interview questions, free resources and certification paths** get saved (4–6% of views).
- **"Power BI is dead / Fabric Apps" hot takes** get the most views.
- **AI on top of Power BI or Fabric** (Claude, agents, vibe-coded reports) is the fastest-rising topic.

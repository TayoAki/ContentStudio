The category is Power BI, Databricks, Microsoft Fabric, data engineering, data analyst, SQL, Snowflake, data science and dashboards. The data comes from the `niche-scripts` skill run on 12 search terms:

| Platform | Posts | Window |
| --- | --- | --- |
| TikTok | 190 | Since Jul 1 2026 |
| Instagram | 56 | Since Jul 1 2026 |
| YouTube | 255 | Search pages carry no dates, so some may be older |

**Scores used:**
- **× followers** (TikTok): views ÷ followers.
- **× median** (YouTube): views ÷ the channel's median over its last 60 uploads.
- **Shares %** and **saves %**: as a share of views.

A big score on a small account means the platform pushed the post to people who don't follow it. That is the viral signal. Shares are what carry a post beyond the people who see it first.

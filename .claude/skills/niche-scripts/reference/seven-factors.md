# The seven factors: a checklist for every short

From a creator's breakdown of 7,356 viral shorts (pasted by the user, Oct 22 2026). Every script this skill writes fills in all seven; a blank is a script that isn't ready.

| # | Factor | What to decide | How this skill does it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Topic** | One sub-niche per video | The niche's 5–7 sub-niches are listed in the research doc; each script names its sub-niche |
| 2 | **Hooks** | Verbal (heard, first 3 s), written (read, first 3 s), visual (seen, first 3 s) | All three, each borrowed from a **5× post** (views ≥ 5× the account's followers); `discover.py` marks them |
| 3 | **Value** | Measurable, not fluffy | A specific, checkable takeaway; at least one line from the presenter's own experience; credit any outside source |
| 4 | **Angle** | One of seven | Tutorial · comparison · myth-bust/common mistake · do vs don't · tip/hack · transformation · challenge |
| 5 | **CTA** | Grow or lead | "Comment WORD": a keyword of 7 letters or fewer, never reused, a real freebie behind it (ManyChat-style auto-DM) |
| 6 | **Format** | Not a sit-down talk | Talking + screen cutaways · whiteboard · green screen · clone (two of you compare) · voice-over · setting change · shot/angle change every ~2 s · reaction · Q&A · props · multitasking |
| 7 | **Editing style** | 2–3 colours, 2–3 fonts, 2–3 visual types, the same every video | The house kit (Editor V1 `ev1_parker.py`, blue): blue ground + yellow key words + white; bold sans for the written hook, a smaller one for captions, an accent for annotations; talking head + screen + graphic slides |

## Rules that come with it

- **Hooks are researched, never invented.** Keep a hook bank per niche: for every 5× post, record:
    - the verbal hook (its first sentence);
    - the written hook (the on-screen text in the first 3 s);
    - the visual hook (what's on screen in the first 3 s).
- **One idea, seven videos.** Run the same topic through the seven angles before writing new topics.
- **Authority beats information.** The presenter's own experience is the value most of the time; a study or doc is support, credited on screen.
- **No views for their own sake.** No memes or trends that won't bring the followers or leads the account is for.
- **Consistency over the lucky post.** The first hit can take weeks. Keep posting.
